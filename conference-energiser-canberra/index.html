What Canberra organisers ask.

Anything not covered here, ask in your enquiry. You will get a straight answer.

Do you come to Canberra?

Yes. WELI is based in Sydney and has hosted or performed in Canberra every year since 2024. He did his PhD at ANU and is a Visiting Fellow there.

What does travel from Sydney add?

It depends on the date and the time of day. Travel appears as its own line in your written quote, so you see the full cost before you book.

Will it suit a public-sector or academic audience?

Yes. Everyone can take part from their seat, standing is optional and nobody is singled out. It fits a changeover between sessions.

What does the venue need to provide?

Very little: a PA suited to the room, one wireless microphone, a playback connection for the backing track and a short soundcheck. A one-page technical brief comes with your quote, ready for the venue's AV team.

Can everyone take part?

Yes. It works seated, standing or with very little movement. Tell us about mobility, hearing, vision or sensory needs when you enquire, and the session is shaped around them.

How do we book?

Send the event, the audience and the moment. Within two business days you get a fixed written quote, with the fee and travel shown separately, plus booking terms. The date is held once you accept in writing and pay the deposit or invoice.
