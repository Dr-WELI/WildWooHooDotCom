WELI to co-host The Visionaries Among Us at ANU

An ANU Alumni showcase. Co-hosted by Dr WELI and Lee Constable.

Dr WELI returns to ANU to co-host The Visionaries Among Us, an alumni showcase, alongside science communicator Lee Constable. Saturday 15 August 2026, free and open to all.

ANU has invited Dr WELI to co-host its alumni showcase, The Visionaries Among Us. The afternoon brings together ANU researchers working at the frontiers of artificial intelligence, astrophysics and Australia's deep history, from its ancient past to its possible futures.

On the bill

  • Dr Ben Swift on artificial intelligence.
  • Professor Brad Tucker and Nobel Laureate Professor Brian Schmidt on astrophysics.
  • Professor Ann McGrath on Australia's deep history.
  • Three-Minute Thesis finalists, pitching bold ideas from across their fields.

The host

Weli hosts as an ANU alumnus (PhD) and a Visiting Fellow at ANU's Centre for the Public Awareness of Science. He is the scientist-artist behind Kangaroo Time, the AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024, and now runs WildWooHoo, the science-to-art studio where he coined sci pop. His job on the day is to keep the science warm, clear and human, so every idea lands with the room.

Why it matters

The event puts frontier research in front of a general audience, and pairs some of ANU's most established names with the researchers coming up behind them. It is the kind of room WildWooHoo is built for: complex ideas, made into something people take part in.

Attend

The Visionaries Among Us is an afternoon of discovery, curiosity and inspiration. It is free and open to all, with registration through ANU.

  • When. Saturday 15 August 2026, 3:00 to 5:00pm.
  • Where. Manning Clark Theatre, ANU, Canberra.
  • Cost. Free. Registration required.
