Open calls and opportunities, August 2026

A short, checked list of open opportunities for the WildWooHoo community. Canberra and Sydney first.

Five open calls worth a look this week: a Canberra drawing prize, a paid Canberra Glassworks residency, Vivid Sydney's 2027 call for ideas, City of Sydney creative grants, and a national Creative Australia grant. Every link below was checked this week and is currently open.

We keep this list short on purpose. We only list what we can verify is real, open now and has a genuine deadline still ahead of it. If that means fewer items some weeks, that is the trade we will keep making.

Canberra and Sydney

M16 Artspace Drawing Prize 2026

Who: artists anywhere in Australia · Deadline: 11:59pm, Sunday 4 October 2026

An open entry drawing prize at M16 Artspace in Canberra, with a prize pool of more than $15,000. Traditional and experimental drawing practices are both welcome. No entry fee for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander artists or Pension Concession Card holders.

Canberra Glassworks, Graduate in Residence 2027

Who: early-career glass practitioners, Australian citizens, permanent residents, or artists based in Australia for six months or more · Deadline: 5 October 2026

A two-year, paid residency at Canberra Glassworks, four days a week with superannuation and leave. Aimed at recent graduates from glass programs, giving early-career artists a real foothold in the field.

Vivid Sydney 2027, Expressions of Interest

Who: artists, designers, producers, architects, engineers, businesses and educational institutions · Deadline: 5:00pm, Friday 28 August 2026

Vivid Sydney is taking pitches for its 2027 festival, running 28 May to 19 June 2027, across the Music, Minds and Food pillars. The Light pillar has already closed. This one is close to its deadline, so do not sit on it.

City of Sydney, Creative Grants 2026-27

Who: professional practicing artists living or working in the City of Sydney and Greater Sydney · Deadline: 4:00pm, Tuesday 1 September 2026 (AEST)

Two open streams: Artist Support, up to $25,000 for a year of practice, and Creative Projects. Both close on the same date, and both are aimed at rebuilding a working creative life in Sydney, not just funding a single output.

Australia

Creative Australia, Arts Projects for Individuals and Groups

Who: individuals and groups, any art practice, anywhere in Australia · Deadline: 3:00pm, Tuesday 1 September 2026 (AEST)

National funding from $10,000 to $50,000 for arts activities and projects running up to two years. Open across all artforms, including work that sits between science and art.

Know of an open call we have missed, especially something local to Canberra? Tell us at hello@wildwoohoo.com and we will check it for the next roundup.
