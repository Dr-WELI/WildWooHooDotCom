Open calls and opportunities, August 2026

A short, checked list of open opportunities for the WildWooHoo community. Canberra and Sydney first.

We checked this list again this week. Six of the eight items we had are now closed or closing too soon to be useful, including the Canberra Glassworks residency, the Kilgour Prize and the Theo Murphy Initiative grants, so we have dropped them. We kept the two still open, and found three newly verified opportunities: a rolling ACT arts grant with no fixed round, a City of Sydney fund for creative space, and a micro-grants round for Western Sydney. We could not verify an international opportunity worth including this week, so this list is all Australian for now.

We keep this list short on purpose. We only list what we can verify is real, open now and has a genuine deadline still ahead of it. If that means fewer items some weeks, that is the trade we will keep making.

Canberra and Sydney

ACT Government, Arts Activities Funding, Up to $5k

Who: artists, groups and organisations based in the ACT · Deadline: rolling, no fixed round. This 2026-27 window closes midnight, 19 May 2027

A standing ACT Government grant for arts activities, from $500 to $5,000. There is no round to wait for: apply any time, at least six weeks ahead of when your project starts.

Create NSW, Regional Arts Touring, Round 2

Who: individual artists, arts workers, groups and organisations touring work across regional NSW · Deadline: 2pm, 13 October 2026

Grants of up to $75,000 for individuals and collectives, and up to $120,000 for organisations, to help an existing show, exhibition or cultural project tour through regional New South Wales.

Creative Western Sydney Micro-Grants, 2026-27

Who: individual artists, collectives and small-to-medium arts organisations working in Western Sydney · Deadline: 2pm, 2 November 2026

Micro-grants of $3,000 to $5,000 for individuals and collectives, and $5,000 to $10,000 for small-to-medium organisations, to develop new creative work in Western Sydney.

City of Sydney, Cultural Infrastructure Grants, 2026-27

Who: organisations and individuals creating or upgrading creative space in the City of Sydney local government area · Deadline: 4pm, 30 June 2027

Funding to set up a new, affordable creative space or improve an existing one. Applications are open now, well ahead of the closing date, so there is no need to rush.

Australia

ANAT Synapse Residency 2027

Who: individual Australian artists who have already lined up a research organisation to host them · Deadline: 3pm ACDT, 14 October 2026

A 16-week paid residency connecting an artist with an Australian research host for open-ended creative work alongside science, with no set outcome required. Artists apply jointly with the host they have already engaged.

Know of an open call we have missed, especially something local to Canberra or Sydney? Tell us at hello@wildwoohoo.com and we will check it for the next roundup.
