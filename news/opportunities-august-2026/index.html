Open calls and opportunities, August 2026

A short, checked list of open opportunities for the WildWooHoo community. Canberra and Sydney first.

We checked this list again this week. The M16 Artspace Drawing Prize closes today, too late to be useful to most readers, so we have dropped it. Everything else held up, and we found five new, verified opportunities: a painting prize in Newcastle, a subsidised artist studio in Bondi, a touring grant for NSW artists, and two national residencies that pair artists with research organisations. Several of these close within the next two days, so move quickly if one is for you.

We keep this list short on purpose. We only list what we can verify is real, open now and has a genuine deadline still ahead of it. If that means fewer items some weeks, that is the trade we will keep making.

Canberra and Sydney

Canberra Glassworks, 2027 Artists in Residence

Who: established glass artists, Australian citizens, permanent residents, or artists based in Australia for six months or more · Deadline: 5 October 2026

An open competitive residency at Canberra Glassworks for artists with real experience working in glass, who can use the studio's equipment safely and independently or already have a plan for skilled support.

Kilgour Prize 2027

Who: professional painters, Australian citizens or residents of 12 months or more · Deadline: 11:59pm, Monday 5 October 2026

A non-acquisitive prize for figurative or portrait painting at Newcastle Art Gallery, back after a five-year break. $50,000 for the winning work, plus a $5,000 People's Choice award. Entry fee is $50.

Waverley Artist Studios

Who: emerging, mid-career and professional artists working in or around Bondi and Sydney's eastern suburbs · Deadline: 11 October 2026

A fully subsidised 12-month studio at the Bondi School of Arts, plus paid professional development such as workshops and artist talks. The residency runs from March 2026 to February 2027.

Create NSW, Regional Arts Touring, Round 2

Who: individual artists, arts workers, groups and organisations touring work across regional NSW · Deadline: 2pm, 13 October 2026

Grants of $10,000 to $120,000 to help an existing show, exhibition or cultural project tour through regional New South Wales.

Australia

National Science Week Public Event Grants, 2027 to 2030

Who: individuals and organisations across Australia · Deadline: 5pm AEDT, 6 October 2026

Grants of $5,000 to $20,000 to run science engagement activities during National Science Week. This round opens funding for the next four years, starting with the 30th National Science Week, 14 to 22 August 2027.

Theo Murphy Initiative, Flagship, Amplify and Participation Support grants

Who: early and mid-career researchers in STEM across Australia · Deadline: 11:59pm AEDT, Monday 5 October 2026

Grants from the Australian Academy of Science for early and mid-career researchers. Flagship grants of up to $50,000 fund larger initiatives, Amplify grants of up to $15,000 fund workshops and mentoring, and Participation Support grants of up to $5,000 help cover the cost of attending a conference or workshop.

ANAT Synapse Residency 2027

Who: individual Australian artists who have already lined up a research organisation to host them · Deadline: 14 October 2026

A 16-week paid residency connecting an artist with an Australian research host for open-ended creative work alongside science, with no set outcome required. Artists apply jointly with the host they have already engaged.

Australian Tapestry Workshop, 2027 Artist in Residence, Open Call Residency

Who: artists based in any Australian state or territory · Deadline: 5pm AEST, 12 October 2026

A residency inside ATW's weaving studio in Melbourne, with access to looms and specialist textile-construction support. Two Open Call places are available for 2027.

Know of an open call we have missed, especially something local to Canberra or Sydney? Tell us at hello@wildwoohoo.com and we will check it for the next roundup.
