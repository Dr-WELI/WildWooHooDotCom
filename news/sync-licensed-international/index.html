International sync placements, three broadcasters

WildWooHoo Studio · rights-cleared catalogue · updated 14 June 2026

Kangaroo Time has been sync-licensed by three international broadcasters since 2024: MSNBC The 11th Hour in the US, UFA Show + Factual in Germany, and Productions Mustang III in Canada. Master clips of all three placements live on disk with cleared sync rights, ready for studio communications and partner pitches.

What sync-licensed actually means

A sync license is the broadcaster's contractual permission to synchronise a piece of music (master + publishing rights) with their visual content. The fee paid for each sync is separate from streaming royalties or live performance royalties. For an emerging-tier music artist, sync placements are a concrete signal to other industry counterparts (festival bookers, A&R, label scouts, music supervisors) that the catalogue is rights-cleared, ready to use, and already trusted by international rights-buyers.

WildWooHoo Studio holds the master + publishing splits on Kangaroo Time and Cabra da Peste, with documented agreements available on request. APRA AMCOS membership is in progress as the studio's standing performing-rights interface.

What three placements actually prove

For sync agents and broadcasters considering the WELI catalogue, the three sync-licensed placements answer four practical questions before any conversation starts.

  • Rights are clean. Three independent international broadcasters cleared the music with WildWooHoo Studio and proceeded. Master + publishing splits are documented.
  • The catalogue translates across formats. News (MSNBC), entertainment quiz (UFA), arts and culture (Mustang III). Three different registers used the same artwork, not as background bed but as the story.
  • International appetite exists. US, Germany, Canada. Different markets, different programming contexts, the same conclusion.
  • The studio is responsive. Requests for masters, additional stems, instrumental mixes, or extended versions are handled directly with cleared turnaround.

What's available

Kangaroo Time and Cabra da Peste (G.O.A.T.) are open for sync. The full Performance set (Kangaroo Time Original, Fireflies, Horse With No Name, Feels Like Summer) sits in 24-bit production masters at the studio. Stems, vocal mixes, instrumentals, and BPM-locked extended versions on request. Formats covered: television, film, documentary, advertising, brand work, sports, news, current affairs.

Note: two of the Performance set tracks are cover masters (A Horse With No Name, Feels Like Summer); APRA AMCOS or publisher clearance is required before any streaming release of those covers. The original-composition tracks have no such restriction.
