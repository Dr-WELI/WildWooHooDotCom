Catalysts, not endpoints

The song and video were not the endpoint of the communication process. They functioned as catalysts for subsequent engagement: interviews, public talks, workshops, performances, science festivals, and educational events.

Examples: the opening plenary at Encontro Anual de Etologia 2024 (one of the largest ethological gatherings in Latin America); the ACT National Science Week 2024 + 2025 programme (funded by Inspiring Australia ACT); a sustained series of institutional and public-facing engagements at drweli.com/program; the launch of WildWooHoo Studio itself (this site). The video was also featured in international entertainment TV, played in nightclubs, sync-licensed for a non-scientific documentary, and performed in multiple cultural festivals locally.
