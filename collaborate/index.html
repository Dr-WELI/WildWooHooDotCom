Partner with us

Partnering with WildWooHoo means partnering with a creative innovation studio, a studio that turns complex ideas into songs, films, books and live sessions. One method runs through all of it, and there are several ways to work with us. Music is the studio's first format, and the same method makes keynotes, workshops and consultancy.

Individual creators are welcome too: artists, scientists and scientist-artists join our register to collaborate on the work, and we work to widen who takes part. The studio was founded by Dr WELI, a behavioural ecologist (PhD, ANU) and AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024.

Creative Research Engagement Partner

A 6 to 12 month retainer where the studio is embedded across an organisation's conferences, public engagement, research translation and staff development. One creative translator on the inside, shaping how your ideas meet people all year. Suits universities, research institutes and cultural organisations with a pipeline of moments across the year.

Book or commission us

Tell us what you want to book or commission and the result you need, and we send a proposal built around it, within two working days. To book WELI in person for a talk, workshop or school visit, you can also enquire on drweli.com. Smaller budgets are welcome, we scale to schools, community and not-for-profits.

Prefer email? Write to hello@wildwoohoo.com. We reply within two working days.

Register

Join the register, our list of collaborators we call on for paid projects, if you want to work with us. We welcome artists, scientists and scientist-artists, and we work to widen who takes part. One short form, reviewed on a rolling basis. If something fits, we will be in touch.

Partner

Programmes, films and music are made with partners, project by project, under contract. Our partners include institutions, funders, broadcasters, publishers, universities, schools and brands. Five ways to partner with us:

Grant funders and philanthropic partners

Arts and science funders across federal, state, philanthropic and international bodies. We carry the application.

Broadcasters and publishers

Children's animation commissions, music documentary, science-led series and illustrated book lists.

Sync and brand licensing

Music for sync placement in television, film, documentary, advertising and brand work, plus library and custom commissions.

Universities and research institutions

ARC Linkage and Discovery impact-pathway partnerships, HDR co-supervision and research-engagement collaborations.

Schools and programme partners

School visits, assemblies and classroom STEAM formats, with ongoing programme partnerships through Kangaroo Time Kids.
