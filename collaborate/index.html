Collaborate

Good work needs the right collaborators and the right partners. They are hard to find at the right moment. We bring people and organisations into our projects. Those projects turn evolutionary biology, ecology and animal behaviour into songs, films, books and school workshops.

This page is where you tell us what you do. Individual creators can join our register. Institutions, funders and commissioners can partner with us project by project. The studio was founded by Dr WELI.

Disciplines

Our projects draw on many crafts. We work with creators across these areas.

  • Music. Producers, songwriters, vocalists and topliners. Alt-pop, Latin and electronic especially.
  • Film and video. Directors, cinematographers and editors for music video, documentary and short-form.
  • Animation and illustration. Animators, illustrators and book designers across our screen and publishing work.
  • Movement. Choreographers and dancers who translate animal behaviour into performance.
  • Photography and styling. Photographers, stylists, art directors, hair-and-make-up artists and models.
  • Research. Behavioural ecologists and field scientists who consult on the science behind the work.
  • Writing and story. Children's and editorial writers, plus short-form and social producers.
  • Design and apps. Designers and developers working on the studio's app ecosystem.

Partner

Programmes, films and music are made with partners. We work project by project, under contract. Our partners include institutions, funders, broadcasters, publishers, universities, schools and brands. There are five ways to partner with us.

Grant funders and philanthropic partners

We work with arts and science funders. They span federal, state, philanthropic and international partners. We carry the application. See the funding partnerships list on Research and Educational.

Broadcasters and publishers

We take on children's animation commissions, music documentary, science-led series and illustrated book lists. See upcoming music work and upcoming educational work.

Sync and brand licensing

Our music is available for sync placement in television, film, documentary, advertising and brand work. We take library and custom commissions. Music-video direction and visual identity work are separate commissions. See sync and licensing.

Universities and research institutions

We work on ARC Linkage and Discovery impact-pathway partnerships. We also take on HDR co-supervision conversations and research-engagement collaborations. Dr WELI is an ANU CPAS Visiting Fellow. See the lines of research.

Schools and programme partners

We offer school visits and assemblies. Classroom STEAM formats and ongoing programme partnerships run through the Kangaroo Time Kids work. WooHoo! Let's Meet community formats are also planned. See programmes delivered.
