Collaborate with WildWooHoo

Two doors. The Creative Register is for individual creators (music, film, animation, photography, design, research) who want to be considered for project work. Partner with us is for institutions, funders, broadcasters, publishers, and commissioners who want to build something together.

CREATIVE REGISTER:how it works

How the
register
works

  • You register your interest. Share links to your work, your discipline, and where you are based.
  • We keep you on the register. The studio reviews the register as projects take shape. We look for craft and fit, not credentials.
  • Projects come to you. When a funded brief fits your discipline and location, we reach out. You opt in or pass.
  • We deliver together. Engagements are agreed project by project and contracted, with proper credit and fair rates on funded work.
  • The register is our capacity. Grant applications cite the register as part of WildWooHoo's delivery capacity. You stay independent; the studio carries the application.

DISCIPLINES:we collaborate across

Disciplines we
collaborate across

  • Music. Producers, songwriters, vocalists and topliners. Alt-pop, Latin, electronic and genre-fluid especially.
  • Film & video. Directors, cinematographers, editors for music video, documentary and short-form.
  • Animation & illustration. Animators, illustrators and book designers across our screen and publishing work.
  • Movement. Choreographers and dancers who translate animal behaviour into embodied performance.
  • Photography & styling. Photographers, stylists, art directors, hair-and-make-up artists and models.
  • Research. Behavioural ecologists and field scientists who consult on the science behind the work.
  • Writing & story. Children's and editorial writers, plus short-form and social producers.
  • Design + apps. Designers and developers working on the studio's app ecosystem (shop, editor, papers, brain orchestrator and siblings).

Geographies prioritised right now: Sydney + Canberra (priority), Brisbane, Melbourne (AU); Berlin, London, Lisbon (EU); São Paulo, Rio, Recife (BR). Other locations are welcome.

PARTNER:institutions + funders

Partner
with us

For institutions, funders, broadcasters, publishers, commissioners, universities, schools, and brands. Different door from the Creative Register. We work project by project under contract; the studio carries the application and the production; partners get cultural reach, evidence-based content, or commissioned creative work.

Partner type 01

Grant funders + philanthropic partners

Federal arts and science, state arts, philanthropic education and climate funders, international research partners. WildWooHoo carries the application; named studio capacity includes the Creative Register, the founder's academic anchor (Dr WELI, ANU CPAS Visiting Fellow), and a documented impact record (Dance Your PhD 2024, Falling Walls Engage 2025 Top 30, 238+ media features).

SEE:R&E funding partnerships list

Partner type 02

Broadcasters + publishers

Children's animation commissions, music documentary, science-led series, illustrated book lists. The studio retains creative and commercial rights on every original work; deal structure varies project by project.

Partner type 03
