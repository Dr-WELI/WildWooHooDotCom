About

WildWooHoo builds creative worlds where nature, emotion and culture meet for social change

We make audio-visual products and experiences that help people feel closer to the natural world and more aware we are an extension of it

Founded by Dr WELI, WildWooHoo explores how music, visual storytelling, and nature-inspired narratives can create new ways of thinking about belonging, diversity, and social change

Impact focus

Two areas of social impact

01

Natural diversity and our relationship with one another

We explore how individual differences, group life, cooperation, and social behaviour shape the way we relate to each other, often using animal societies as a mirror for human life

02

Changing environments and our belonging to nature

We create work that reconnects people with the understanding we are part of the animal kingdom and part of nature, especially in times of environmental change, uncertainty, and disconnection

Creative process

How we create

01

Nature as muse

We begin with natural behaviour, animal societies, ecosystems, or environmental feeling

02

Emotion as entry point

We shape music, imagery, rhythm, and story so audiences feel first, then reflect

03

Interconnected storytelling

Human and non-human worlds are placed in relation to one another to create recognition and social meaning

04

Creative work with message

The outcome can become music, music videos, educational concepts, science communication, or visual experiences carrying social impact

Our work aims to bring humans closer to the animal kingdom

About
the studio

Nature · Emotion · Culture

WildWooHoo builds creative worlds where nature, emotion, and culture meet for social change. We make audio-visual work that helps people feel closer to the natural world — and more aware that we are an extension of it.

Founded by

Dr WELI

Brazilian-Australian artist, PhD ecologist (ANU), and the creative director, science expert, face, and voice of WildWooHoo. Winner of Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024.

drweli.com →

Creative practice

Research → Story → Culture

Behavioural ecology, animal social systems, and environmental feeling become music, film, and learning formats. Emotion is the entry point; reflection follows.

Why it travels

Popular culture as the bridge

We work in the formats audiences already love — pop songs, music videos, kids' shows — so research arrives without academic friction.

Two areas
of impact

What our work moves the dial on

Every project lives in one of these two zones — sometimes both. They're the studio's compass.

Impact / 01

Natural diversity & how we relate to one another

Individual differences, group life, cooperation, and social behaviour — animal societies as a mirror for human life. Recognition, belonging, coexistence.

Impact / 02

Changing environments & our belonging to nature

We make work that reconnects people with the understanding that we are part of the animal kingdom — especially in times of environmental change, uncertainty, and disconnection.

How
we create

A four-step studio logic

Same logic across every output — song, video, picture book, kids' series. Nature in, culture out.

Step 01

Nature as muse

Begin with natural behaviour, animal societies, ecosystems, or environmental feeling.

Step 02

Emotion as entry

Shape music, imagery, rhythm, story so audiences feel first — then reflect.

Step 03

Interconnected worlds

Human and non-human worlds in dialogue — recognition, reflection, social meaning.

Step 04

Creative work with message

Songs, music videos, educational formats, science communication — all carrying the impact.

Our work brings humans closer to the animal kingdom.
