What kids
take away

  • Curiosity about animals, ecosystems, and how the natural world works
  • A sense of belonging — recognising themselves as part of the animal kingdom
  • Emotional fluency around diversity, individuality, and coexistence
  • Joy in observing, asking, and connecting with nature
  • Confidence to share what they notice — in their own voice
