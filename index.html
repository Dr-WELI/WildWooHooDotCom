WildWooHoo, a science-art studio in Sydney

wildwoohoo

what we evolved for

WildWooHoo is a science-art studio in Sydney. We turn evolutionary biology, ecology and animal behaviour into songs, films, books and school workshops.

Problem

Most research stays inside the academy. It rarely arrives in a form people can feel. We build the entry points that carry it into everyday culture.

Animal behaviour, sociality and evolution are the source. Popular culture is the surface. Despite all we have built, we are still part of the animal kingdom.

Wings

Music + Entertainment

We write and produce music that carries behavioural ecology into pop culture.

Research + Educational

We make research-led children's media, school workshops and public engagement.

Work

Kangaroo Time is the studio's first major music work. It turns PhD research on wild eastern grey kangaroos into a song and film. The dance cast drew its styles from multicultural Canberra.

It is the AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024.

Founder

WildWooHoo was founded by Dr WELI, a behavioural ecologist and recording artist. His full biography and work live at drweli.com.
