A studio
for nature × pop

What we do · Why we exist

WildWooHoo is a creative music studio that turns behavioural research, Australian nature, and ecological feeling into songs, videos, and learning formats audiences actually want to live with. Culture first, science underneath.

Founded by

Dr WELI

Brazilian-Australian artist · PhD ecologist (ANU) · creative director, science expert, face and voice of WildWooHoo. Winner of Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024.

drweli.com →

Creative practice

Research → Song → Culture

Behavioural ecology, animal social systems, and environmental feeling become music, film, and learning formats. Emotion is the entry point; reflection follows.

Why it travels

Pop is the bridge

We work in formats audiences already love — pop songs, music videos, kids' shows. Research arrives without academic friction, riding contemporary culture.

Two areas
of impact

What our work moves the dial on

Every project lives in one of these two zones — sometimes both. They're the studio's compass.

Impact / 01

Natural diversity & how we relate

Individual differences, group life, cooperation, social behaviour. Animal societies as a mirror for human life. Recognition, belonging, coexistence.

Impact / 02

Changing environments & belonging to nature

We make work that reconnects people with the understanding that we are part of the animal kingdom — especially in times of environmental change, uncertainty, and disconnection.

How
we create

Four-step studio logic

Same logic across every output — song, video, picture book, kids' series. Nature in, culture out.

Step 01

Nature as muse

Begin with natural behaviour, animal societies, ecosystems, environmental feeling.

Step 02

Emotion as entry

Shape music, imagery, rhythm, story so audiences feel first — then reflect.

Step 03

Interconnected worlds

Human and non-human worlds in dialogue. Recognition, reflection, social meaning.

Step 04

Work with message

Songs, music videos, kids' formats, science communication — each carrying the impact.
