science into popular culture

A Sydney science-to-art studio and the home of sci pop.

We turn knowledge about nature, people and technology into music, film and creative experiences.

Some of that work is commissioned by organisations. Some begins inside the studio and grows through artists, funders and partners. The two pathways are separate below.

The work that started the method

Kangaroo Time

A PhD on kangaroo personality became a pop song, music film and multicultural dance work. Its reception led WELI to define sci pop: contemporary pop carrying science as aesthetic, theme or method.

AAAS Dance Your PhD Overall Winner 2024 · 238+ media features across five continents.
