WildWooHoo

WildWooHoo is a creative studio for the animal kingdom - including us. We create music, audiovisuals and educational materials that draw parallels between animal and human societies. Animal behaviour, sociality and evolution are our edge; witty songwriting, popular culture, traditions, and the ecosystems we share give the work its breath. Despite all the worlds we have built, biologically we remain part of the animal kingdom.

We don't make work about the wild. We make work that comes from it. From behavioural research, animal societies, ecological change, evolutionary biology, and the parallels between human and non-human life. Hierarchy. Identity. Belonging. Coexistence. Care. Change. The questions the animal kingdom asks loudest are the questions our societies are quietly asking too.

Songs, music videos, animated series, books, classroom moments, public performances. We work in whatever form will travel, hoping the work enters mainstream music and entertainment, because cultural reach is what turns an idea into social impact.

Founded by Dr WELI, Brazilian-Australian artist and behavioural ecologist (PhD, ANU). Winner of Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024. Falling Walls Engage 2025 Top 30 worldwide. WildWooHoo has carried this work to 250+ media features across six continents.

We are a home of creation, not a service studio. Our own projects, our own audience, our own path to impact. We welcome creative collaborators, research partners, commissioners, and funders who want to build something the world actually feels.

Meet the founder

Dr WELI — behavioural ecologist, recording artist, storyteller

Brazilian born, Sydney based. Weliton Menário Costa designs cultural experiences, workshops, and programs through music, movement, and storytelling — building bridges between science, performance, and public culture.

WildWooHoo is his creative studio: where a PhD on kangaroo personality (ANU) becomes songs, videos, animation, books, and public moments that reach far beyond the lab. The flagship work, Kangaroo Time, won Science Magazine’s Dance Your PhD 2024 and travelled through 250+ outlets worldwide. Falling Walls Engage 2025 Top 30, for transforming science into compelling public communication.

The lens stays the same across every form: animal behaviour, sociality, and evolution — refracted through witty songwriting, popular culture, traditions, and the ecosystems we share.
