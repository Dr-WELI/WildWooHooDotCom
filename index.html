What we are

A creative innovation studio. We are not a production company. We lead the creative direction of your production. We do the thinking: the ideas, the references and the narrative, on work that has to carry a complex subject.

Dr WELI is an award-winning creative director who weaves scientific thinking into the making, alongside aesthetics and themes from science and science fiction, and multicultural and other cultural references, chosen to suit what you are trying to say.

Three ways that reaches you

  • Creative direction, for hire. We lead the creative direction of your production, working with your team and respecting your budget. When you need the production made as well, our curated and approved partners deliver it. Commission creative direction
  • Speaking and performance, for hire. Keynotes, workshops, energisers and live sci pop sets. Book us live
  • Our own projects. The studio directs its own work and releases it through WELI, the artist, with books, kits and other products through the shop. Funders and partners back these. Back a project

Signature programs · bookings open

Turn friction into belonging, and expertise into art that connects

Diverse teams where difference creates more friction than belonging. Research and expertise that never reach the people they are for. Dr WELI's two signature programs turn both into something people take part in: How To Lead a Diverse Team Into Belonging, and How To Turn Your Knowledge Into Art That Connects. Both run on his own method, built from behavioural-ecology research, and are booked as keynotes, workshops and cohorts for organisations, universities and the public sector.

Work

Kangaroo Time is WELI's music video, made before the studio. It is a finished project that shows the method in action, and its sci pop method grew from it. It shows the method run all the way through to a finished song, and music is the studio's first format. It turns PhD research on the personality and social behaviour of wild eastern grey kangaroos into a song and film. The dance cast drew its styles from multicultural Canberra, so the work carries belonging and difference as it carries the science.

The framework behind the studio's sci pop music is set out in its founding paper, Sci pop: science as aesthetic, theme and method in contemporary popular music (open access, Zenodo, CC BY 4.0).

It is the AAAS Dance Your PhD Overall Winner 2024.
