WildWooHoo

WildWooHoo is a creative studio for nature, behaviour, and the lives we share — and a home for the projects, partners, and people that translate them into culture.

We don't make music about the wild. We make work that comes from it: from behavioural research, animal societies, environmental change, and the parallels between human and non-human life. Hierarchy. Identity. Belonging. Coexistence. Change. The questions ecology asks loudest are the questions our societies are quietly asking too.

Songs, music videos, animated series, books, classrooms, public moments. We work in whatever form will travel — because cultural reach is how an idea becomes social impact.

Founded by Dr WELI — Brazilian-Australian artist and PhD ecologist, Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024 winner, and Falling Walls Engage 2025 Top 30 worldwide. WildWooHoo has carried this work to 250+ media features across six continents.

We are a home of creation — not a service studio. Our own projects, our own audience, our own path to impact. We welcome creative collaborators, research partners, commissioners, and funders who want to build something the world actually feels.
