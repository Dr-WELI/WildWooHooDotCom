WildWooHoo

For most of music's history, the wild stayed outside the studio. Nature was a setting, not a source. The work that mattered most came from songwriters writing about themselves, for themselves, in cities that don't notice their birds.

Not here.

WildWooHoo is a music studio built on a different premise: that the most interesting music in the world is sitting inside ecological research, animal behaviour, and the lives we share with other species — and that pop, video, and storytelling are the right vehicles to carry it.

We start with research. We translate through music. We aim for cultural reach. Every song is a research project that becomes a feeling.

Founded by Dr WELI — Brazilian-Australian artist, PhD ecologist, Dance Your PhD 2024 winner — WildWooHoo proves the case across 250+ media features: nature × pop travels further than either does alone.
