FORWARD:research interests

What a Dr WELI x WildWooHoo lab would explore: the intersection of behavioural ecology and cultural cognition. What an evolved primate brain does in a world it was not shaped for, and what popular culture does with that mismatch.

  • Human-animal cognition + social-system parallels. Where behavioural-ecology constructs (personality, social rank, group decision-making, maternal effects) describe humans as rigorous comparative biology, not anthropomorphism.
  • Cultural cognition + routine thinking. The "thought templates" transmitted culturally and learned young, leading to biases about longevity, evolution, nature, and the species we share the planet with. Includes science-society conflict cases.
  • Collective imaginaries and emotion in music and popular culture. How the search for higher intelligence, our place in the universe, and the anxiety of feeling alone in it sit against the appreciation of what is readily observable around us, and how that imbalance shapes our relationship with nature, with other species, and sometimes with one another.
  • Evolved brain in synthetic environments. A primate brain shaped over 300,000 years of pre-civilisational life now running in a 15-year-old screen-mediated environment. The mismatch.
