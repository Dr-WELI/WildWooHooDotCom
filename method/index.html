The problem

Standard talks lose the room and are forgotten fast. Our sessions are participatory: the audience is active, and leaves with one clear message.

What you can book

  • Keynote. A conference opener or closer the audience joins.
  • Energiser. A short reset that gets a tired room up and moving.
  • Workshop. Research communication, STEAM, or creative thinking for teams.
  • Performance. A live sci pop set built around your theme.

Our two signature workshops, How To Lead a Diverse Team Into Belonging and How To Turn Your Knowledge Into Art That Connects, are set out in full on our programs page.

How it runs

  1. Open with a short group movement.
  2. Introduce the idea and its human angle.
  3. Translate it into song, story or movement.
  4. Run a group activity.
  5. Close on one clear message.

Why you need us

Delivered by Dr WELI, a behavioural ecologist (PhD, ANU) and recording artist. The method has run from school classrooms to conference plenaries. AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024; 238+ media features across five continents.

Who it is for

Conference producers and event organisers. People-and-culture and L&D teams. University researcher-development and engagement teams. Festival and science-week programmers. Schools. NGOs and community events.

Book us live

Indicative, and quoted to your brief. Fees scale with the organisation and the audience:

  • School sessions from about $549 a session.

Individuals and small groups sit at the lower end. Tell us your budget and audience, and we send a fixed quote within two working days.
