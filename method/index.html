What you hire us for

The Experience

The live session you hire us for: a keynote, energiser (a short session that gets a tired audience up and moving, a mid-afternoon reset), workshop or performance where your audience takes part.

This is the experience you get when you hire us, for an energiser, a workshop, a keynote or a performance. Whatever the format, it runs on one signature: a complex idea turned into something the audience feels, takes part in and remembers.

Most people explain. We translate. We turn the idea into a song, film, story or movement people can feel and take part in. An idea that lives in a paper or a strategy document becomes something the people at your event can feel, move to and make together. People remember what they experience.

Below is the rationale that governs it: the five steps every piece runs through, and the principles we hold to whoever we are working with.

Two signature programs

The same method powers two signature programs, delivered by Dr WELI: How To Lead a Diverse Team Into Belonging, and How To Turn Your Knowledge Into Art That Connects. Both run on Membrane Thinking, his design method built from behavioural-ecology research.

See the two signature programs, delivered as keynotes, workshops and cohorts for organisations, universities and the public sector.

The five steps

Every live experience runs the same five steps, whether it is an energiser, a workshop, a keynote or a performance.

  1. Start together, in movement. We open with something collective, usually dance or shared movement, everyone in the audience doing the same thing and pushing out of static together. That is what unlocks creativity and brings people right in, and it comes before any idea. It is the real difference.
  2. Bring in the idea. With the audience open, we introduce the idea, a research finding, a concept, a message, and find the human angle in it. People remember what they feel.
  3. Translate it. We carry the idea into another form, music, movement, story or image, the one that lets people feel it. Most people explain. We translate. We turn the idea into a song, film, story or movement people can feel and take part in.
  4. Make it together. People take part and make something with the idea: a line, a move, a metaphor. Taking part is what makes it stay.
  5. Leave one clear thing. People walk away with a single idea they keep, and can act on.

The worked example

The method grew from a real piece of work. Kangaroo Time began as WELI's PhD research on kangaroo behaviour and ran through all five steps, before the studio existed. It found its human angle, that kangaroos have personalities and social lives much like ours; it was translated from data into a pop song and a film; it brought people in through a dance cast and a hook that stays in your head; and it left one clear idea an audience could carry home.

The result: AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024, with coverage across 238+ outlets. Read the Kangaroo Time story.
