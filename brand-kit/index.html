WildWooHoo Brand Kit / v6.0

The brand system, as it stands today

A working spec of the WildWooHoo brand as built into the live site on 2026-06-06. The gray metallic chip as the brand mark. Two wordmark variants for two moments of the page narrative. The 14-anchor palette extracted from the chip itself. The type stack, the music + motion system, and the system components that hold the whole thing together.

[ 01 / LOGO SYSTEM ]

The chip is the brand mark

A dark rounded-pebble silhouette with four codex elements set on it: a magenta triangle (reads as the W), a magenta dot (the i), a cyan dot (one O) and a yellow dot (the other O). Those four marks ARE the wordmark, compressed into a chip. Header at 56 by 56 pixels. Favicon at 16, 32 and 180. The apple touch icon. No other primary mark.

[ 02 / WORDMARK ]

Two wordmarks, two moments

The site uses the wordmark in two distinct forms at two distinct moments. Lowercase Caveat with a yellow marker highlight is the splash hero. Outlined cream serif with a rainbow shine sweep is the scroll-up reveal section. Same word, two different surfaces in the page narrative.

[ 02A / SPLASH HERO ]

wildwoohoo

When to use

The first thing the visitor sees, full bleed over the leap photo sequence on the splash hero. Reads as the studio's handwritten signature. Centred, large, slow breathing animation.

Recipe

Caveat 700, lowercase, clamp(72px, 14vw, 220px). Yellow marker highlight band behind the text (linear gradient of #F0E572 at 62 to 82 percent opacity, banded mid-height). Glow halo on top: sage + yellow drop shadows. The Kangaroo Time signature look.

[ 02B / WHAT WE EVOLVED FOR REVEAL ]

WildWooHoo what we evolved for

When to use

First section after the splash. Visitor scrolls up and the outlined wordmark rises into view with the rainbow shine sweeping continuously side to side, and the slogan sits beneath as a caption.

Recipe

Outlined Libre Baskerville 700 (or the bundled brand-kit/logos/wordmark-glow.svg), cream stroke, transparent fill, always-on glow halo (sage + yellow + magenta drop shadows). Rainbow shine layer animates at 4.8s ease-in-out infinite. Slogan is Syne Italic 800, yellow accent on for.

[ 06 / MUSIC + MOTION ]

The site is cinematic and musical

The home page is timed to the track. Splash photos cut on beat. The wordmark cycles on a BPM-locked schedule. Transitions are alien-tech glitch. Below: the parts of the system you can specify and tune.

Track / drives the timing

Kangaroo Time (Instrumental)

The studio's flagship instrumental. Drives every BPM-locked element on the home page: the splash showreel cuts, the wordmark blink cycle, the chromatic transition timing, the music button pulse.

123.78 BPM / E minor / 484.7ms per beat SOURCE: /Kangaroo Time (Instrumental)_24 Bit 44.1 Master.wav

Music button / floats bottom-left

At restMagenta border, magenta glyph, faint magenta glow. Quiet invitation.
PlayingCyan border, cyan glyph, pulsing cyan glow at BPM. Pause icon swaps in.

Leap photo sequence / splash hero

Hard-cut alternating kangaroo / WELI photos behind the wordmark, full bleed. Cuts sync to three audio cues:

  • 01 / BPM HALF-BARS
  • 02 / SAX BAND PEAKS (mid)
  • 03 / VIOLIN BAND PEAKS (hi)

Photos hold and breathe between cuts. Cinematic grade applied: saturate 0.62, contrast 1.18, brightness 0.88.

Cut transition / alien-tech glitch

320ms 5-phase glitch on every leap cut. Reads as a CRT switching transmissions, with the brand palette bleeding through the chromatic split.

  • 00%   Frame slams in, fully desaturated. Chromatic split max (cyan -6px, magenta +6px). Brightness 2x.
  • 12%   Split holds wide. Slight skew. Scanlines peak.
  • 28%   Colour begins to bleed back. Split narrows (-3 / +3). Yellow bleeds in at the bottom edge.
  • 58%   Split down to 1px. Saturation returning. Scan-roll exits.
  • 100% Settled. 0.62 opacity. Cinematic grade hold state.

Wordmark blink cycle / runs while music plays

The lowercase Caveat splash wordmark swaps between two texts on a BPM-locked schedule. Each swap fires a 360ms chromatic glitch split. The wordmark IS the manifesto question.

[ 07 / HEADER + NAV ]

Header system, chip plus burger

The top bar is fixed and minimal. Chip on the left. Long asymmetric hamburger lines on the right. Inline nav and the Start a conversation CTA both live INSIDE the fullscreen dropdown. The header auto-hides while music plays.

Hamburger / 3 long asymmetric lines

Long sage-light hairlines, right-aligned, asymmetric (130 / 92 / 64 px max widths). Drop-shadow halo so they read on busy photo backgrounds. Hover pushes them wider and cyan. Open state morphs to an equal-width X.

Dropdown / fullscreen blur

Click the burger and the screen fills with a 24px blur backdrop and a column of nav links: Music & Entertainment / Research & Educational / Studio / Collaborate, each in Libre Baskerville 700 at clamp(28px, 5.5vw, 64px). Start a conversation drops in as a mono pill at the bottom.

Music & Entertainment Research & Educational Studio Collaborate Start a conversation

Auto-hide / when music plays

After 3.5s of music playing, the header retreats off-screen so the cinematic view dominates. Reveals on cursor in the top 80 pixels, on any mousemove, or on pause. Position is fixed so the chip and burger never scroll away.

Floating [ABOUT] / top-left

Mono JetBrains Mono label, 11px, 0.32em letter-spacing, sage-light with strong dark drop-shadow. Click reveals a 1-paragraph body in Libre Baskerville 400 below it, with italic-yellow behavioural ecology as the accent phrase. No panel, no background, just text over the photo with a halo so it reads.

[ 08 / SYSTEM COMPONENTS ]

The pieces, everywhere visible

Persistent UI that holds the brand across every page: the universe canvas backdrop, the TRANSMITTING chip, the refined cursor, and the welcome prompt that sets the mood on first load.

Universe canvas backdrop

Body-mounted starfield plus scanlines plus a magenta CRT haze. Position fixed at inset:0, z-index 0. Sits underneath every page so the whole site reads as a single dark canvas. Transparent sections let it pass through.

TRANSMITTING chip / top-right

Fixed positioned. Mono uppercase. Cyan blink dot at 1.6s. Reads as a system status indicator that the site is live and broadcasting. Sits beside the language switcher on home.

TRANSMITTING

Cursor / refined ring + dot

Sage-light hairline ring with a small filled dot at the centre. Halo glow. Contextual labels appear next to it on interactive elements: View on images, Watch on videos, Start on the music button. Falls back to system cursor on inputs.

VIEW

Welcome prompt / centred

Cyan-glow command panel centred in the viewport on first load. Three lines of mono micro-copy and a glowing pill. Stays visible (dimmed) after the user presses play, so it reads as an ambient aesthetic element.
