[ 01 / The mark ]

The chip is the brand mark

A rounded-square device tile with a stylised W monogram at the centre, flanked by cyan and yellow dots and crowned by a magenta triangle. HUD microtext sits around the inner border. The canonical chip is emerald green on dark. Ten finish variants extend the chip across contexts.

[ 02 / The wordmark ]

WildWooHoo, open-outline

Outlined geometric monoline lettering. Two parallel hairlines tracing the borders of a thick stroke. The leftmost W and the rightmost o carry slot cuts on their outer edges, with an inward projection inside the letter, mirroring the chip edge. Cream is canonical. [ 03 / The font system ]

Three canonical voices, six accents

Big Shoulders Display, Libre Baskerville and Montserrat carry the brand. Caveat, DM Serif Display and Instrument Serif are kept for editorial accents. Comfortaa, Syne and JetBrains Mono are later additions for the tagline, slogan and HUD microtext.

Big Shoulders

Big Shoulders Display

700 / 800 / 900

Display. Hero headlines, poster type, slide titles, big labels. The closest free typeface to the brand W outline DNA. Token: --font-mark.

Libre Baskerville

Libre Baskerville

400 / 700

Serif. Section headlines, long-form body that wants to feel like a magazine. Project titles in modal pop-ups. The pillar h2 family. Token: --font-serif.

Montserrat

Montserrat

400 / 500 / 700

Sans body. Body text, navigation, UI labels, tags. Anywhere the brand voice is operational rather than expressive. Token: --font-sans.

Script accent. Handwritten emphasis, captions under photography, signature lines, the personal-voice moment. The splash wordmark on the live site is set in Caveat 700 lowercase with a yellow marker highlight. Token: --font-script.

DM Serif Display

DM Serif Display

Regular / italic

Editorial serif. Display headlines that want serif gravity. Kept from v3 for editorial layouts, secondary to Big Shoulders. Token: --font-display.

Emphasis italic. One emphasised word inside a serif headline. Pairs with Libre Baskerville. The yellow-glow italic word in section h2s lives here. Token: --font-italic.

Comfortaa

Tagline voice. Rounded geometric sans, sits in typographic family with the outlined wordmark. Reserved for the tagline lockup. Sage-light body, sage-bright on the closing for.

JetBrains Mono

400 - 700

HUD microtext. Corner labels, system chips, tag-cluster monospace labels, the readouts inside the chip itself. Always uppercase, wide letter-spacing. Never as body, never as headline.

[ 06 / Clear space + minimum size ]

Give it room to breathe

Maintain clear space of at least the chip height on all sides of the lockup. No other type, mark, or graphic sits inside that zone. Minimums protect legibility at small sizes.

1 x chip height 1 x chip height 1 x chip height 1 x chip height

Clear space

At least one chip height of clear space on every side of the lockup. No type, no other marks, no edge of an image inside that zone. The dashed outer rectangle in the diagram shows the safe perimeter.

Minimum sizes

16 px chip mark, minimum

240 px full lockup, minimum width

Below these sizes the inner monogram, the dots, and the HUD microtext start to blur. Use the chip alone, not the lockup, for any surface below 240 pixels wide.

Do not

Do not recolour the chip body outside the listed variants. Do not change the wordmark proportions. Do not replace the tagline font. Do not rearrange the vertical order of the lockup.
