The story in one paragraph

Parallel worlds, one body

WildWooHoo is a creative studio for the animal kingdom - including us. We draw parallels between animal and human societies through music, audiovisuals and educational materials. The brand is built around three visual ideas: a single unified glyph that reads as W, H, and two double-o pairs at once (also a crown, also glasses, one o becomes a musical note); a horizon-cut tile with semicircular pinches at the midline that signals parallel worlds before the eye even reads the mark; and a strict monochrome-at-rest, full-spectrum-at-interaction colour rule. Colour appears only in atmosphere - the chromatic aberration baked into the mark - and in motion - the wordmark's hover shine, the click-burst halo, the radial bloom on share cards.

The mark

One tile, one signal

A black horizon-cut tile with a soft semicircular pinch on each side at its midline. Inside it, a single cream glyph reads as W, H, and two oo pairs at once - read another way, it's a crown; another, a pair of glasses; one of the o's becomes a music note. Embossed in 3D, with a faint chromatic aberration around the stroke edges and a soft halo around the figure. One mark - we collapsed the earlier v4 four-variant set (black / inverted / green / rainbow-rim) into this single primary; the chromatic aberration and embossed surface already carry the full brand palette in trace amounts, so a separate "rainbow" variant duplicated the message.

The wordmark

WildWooHoo — outlined

Drawn as outlined geometric monoline letters. Every letter shows two parallel hairlines tracing the borders of a thick stroke — interior is transparent so background images read through. The two Ws and the H share the heavy stroke from the monogram; the lowercase i, l, d, o use a lighter stroke. H is drawn at the same visual width as W so the caps read with equal weight.

The cardinal rule

Never together

The mark and the wordmark are never used in the same composition. The W in the wordmark already plays the role of the mark — pairing them duplicates the message and weakens both. If a context needs "an icon and a name," use the wordmark only. If it needs just an icon (favicon, app tile, profile picture), use the mark only.

Typography

Three families, one voice

Three geometric sans-serifs in a strict role assignment. Comfortaa carries every heading (small and big alike); Syne carries the rainbow-highlighted accent words; Inter carries every paragraph and label. Each font owns one job. Nothing else.

Headings · Comfortaa · 700

Animal kingdom, music, social impact.

For every heading on the site, regardless of size — h1 page-band heroes, h2 section heads, h3 sub-heads, the "Work with us" CTA. Comfortaa's circular "o"s mirror the wordmark's WildWooHoo — direct visual rhyme with the brand mark.

Rainbow accent · Syne Italic · 800

social impact

Only used for the one emphasised word inside a Comfortaa heading. Always paired with the brand spectrum gradient — Syne's sharp contemporary character creates a deliberate tension against the rounded Comfortaa base. The rainbow word is the moment of brightness.

Body / UI · Inter · 400 / 500 / 600

Despite all the worlds we have built, biologically we remain part of the animal kingdom. We start in animal behaviour, ecology and evolution and translate it through music, music videos, animation, books, and public performance.

For body copy, navigation, UI labels, lists, captions. Industry-standard screen-optimised geometric sans — neutral, crisp, gets out of the way of the brand's two voice fonts above.

Installing the fonts

All five primary families are free Google Fonts (plus Big Shoulders Display + Libre Baskerville as legacy editorial alternates). For the web, paste this into <head>:

<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com">
<link rel="preconnect" href="https://fonts.gstatic.com" crossorigin>
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Comfortaa:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Syne:wght@500;600;700;800&family=Inter:wght@400;500;600;700&family=Instrument+Serif:ital@0;1&family=Caveat:wght@500;700&display=swap">

For Keynote / PowerPoint / Pages / Word, download each .ttf from fonts.google.com/specimen/<family-name> and install on your system. All five primary families are pre-loaded in Canva. The brand's body baseline is Inter; if your tool lacks Inter, Montserrat is the historic fallback.

How the mark and the wordmark talk

One body, many readings

They share one principle and one device:

The mark

The whole brand compressed into a single tile. One glyph is W and H and oo, crown and glasses, sometimes a music note. The horizon-cut silhouette signals "parallel worlds meeting" before the eye even reads the figure inside.

The wordmark

The same principle stretched across a word. Two outlined Ws, a heavy H, and four o's - every letter is two parallel hairlines, like a stroke that's been outlined. H is drawn at the same width as W so the caps read with equal weight.

The spectrum

Stays out of both at rest. Appears in atmosphere (the chromatic aberration baked into the mark, the soft halo around small instances) and in motion (the wordmark's hover shine, the click-burst, the radial bloom on the share card). One palette, multiple presences.

The tension

Monochrome at rest, rainbow at contact. Quiet most of the time, alive in the moments of contact. That's the whole brand.

For Canva, Gmail, presentations & sharing

Quick start in every tool

Canva (Pro recommended)

Upload the SVGs (wordmark) and PNGs (monogram) to your Brand Kit → Logos. The wordmark SVGs scale without quality loss; the monogram at 2048 PNG is plenty for any layout. Paste the hex codes from §Colour into your Brand Kit → Colours. All five primary brand fonts (Comfortaa, Syne, Inter, Instrument Serif, Caveat) are in Canva's library — search by name.

Google Slides / Docs

Insert → Image → Upload from computer. Slides accepts SVG since 2023 (Docs prefers PNG). For body text, change theme fonts to Inter (body) + Comfortaa (headings) via the "more fonts" picker. The rainbow accent text effect in this kit is web-only - in Slides, set the accent italic word in Syne with a solid amber (#B07F30) or savanna (#27A05B) fill instead.

Keynote / PowerPoint / Pages / Word

Use the PNG version (these apps don't render SVG with full fidelity). Use the 2048 monogram PNG or 4800 wordmark PNG for highest quality. Install all six font families from Google Fonts on the system first.

Figma

Drop the SVGs into a new file. The mask-based wordmark renders with full outline fidelity. Set up Figma Colour styles using the hex codes from §Colour, and Text styles using the families from §Typography.

Gmail signature

Upload the 1200-wide wordmark PNG to your domain (or any image host). Reference the URL in your Gmail signature image. Recommended max 200px tall on display. Pair with system fonts for body text — custom fonts don't carry across email clients.

Sharing the whole kit

This brand-kit/ directory is self-contained. Zip it (zip -r wildwoohoo-brand-kit.zip brand-kit/) and email or upload to Drive / Dropbox / WeTransfer. Recipients get every file plus the README.md reference doc and this visual page.

For registering the brand

The mark and the wordmark are two separate intellectual works — register them as separate trademarks for maximum protection.

Submit monogram-glow.png (the 2048-px master) or monogram-glow.svg (PNG-wrapped). Inside the tile is a unified cream-coloured glyph combining the letters W, H, and two double-o pairs in a single connected outline, with a soft chromatic aberration around the stroke edges." Class 9 (digital / audiovisual goods) and/or Class 41 (entertainment, education services).
