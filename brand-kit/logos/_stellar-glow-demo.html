Stellar-glow — wordmark halo, reimagined

Proposal only — none of this is wired into the live site. Four candidate treatments that turn the flat "rainbow" halo into a burning-star / sound-speed / technological glow. The trick across all four: keep the brand spectrum stops, but pull them toward a white-hot core with the lavender/green pushed out to a faint corona, then add motion that reads as a signal travelling. Hover the sweep and corona cards.

1 · Star core (static, recommended baseline)

Stacked drop-shadows on a burning-star ramp — tight white-hot centre, amber bloom, faint cool corona. Drop-in replacement for the current halo, no markup change.

2 · Sound-speed pulse (animated)

The corona breathes on a ~3.2s ease — a star twinkling / a slow sonar ping. Respects prefers-reduced-motion.

3 · Signal sweep (hover)
