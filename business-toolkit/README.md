## Operating model

The primary buyer is a conference producer, university/research team or public-sector/innovation programme owner. The primary buying unit is a 5–20 minute live programme intervention: opening, mid-programme reset or closing.

The commercial path is:

```text
Qualified enquiry → optional fit call → recommendation → fixed quote → terms → deposit/invoice → confirmed date → production brief
```

The website does not collect card details or promise instant date availability. Xero should handle formal Australian quotes/invoices and Stripe Payment Links can handle an accepted deposit or balance once the final payment setup is configured.

## 90-day operating rhythm

Discover and qualify events early, then send 3–5 tailored approaches per week. Record source, buyer, event, proposed insertion point, response, next action and outcome. Keep human approval before any external contact.

## Important placeholders to complete before sending externally

- Confirm the minimum acceptable fee and deposit percentage.
- Confirm GST status and cancellation/postponement terms.
- Replace case-study placeholders with approved facts and permissions.
- Confirm the exact WELI role for each named event.
- Gather licensed footage before publishing a new event reel.
