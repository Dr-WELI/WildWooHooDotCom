## Outreach rules

- Personalise the first paragraph with a real programme detail.
- Suggest one insertion point, not every service.
- Ask whether the programme is still being shaped.
- Use official submission, partnership or sponsorship routes when provided.
- Keep a human review before sending.
- Record date, recipient, source, response and next action.
- Do not claim a budget, open slot or relationship that has not been verified.
