A studio is a network.

WildWooHoo's project work is built with a curated, geographically-distributed pool of music, video, animation, design, and research collaborators. Members are vetted by application. We bring projects to the network; the network helps us deliver, win grants, and travel further than a private studio ever could.

Application-only · curated, not crowdsourced Project briefs shared with members first Active in AU · EU · Brazil

How it
works.

  • You apply. Fill out the form below, links to your work, role, location, what you want to collaborate on.
  • We review. Dr WELI personally reviews every application. We're looking for quality, fit, and craft, not credentials.
  • You're invited in. Approved members get access to the private Slack, the Notion resources area, and the brief board.
  • Projects come to you. When a brief fits your skills and location, we reach out. You opt in or pass.
  • We apply for funding together. Grant applications cite the network as part of WildWooHoo's delivery capacity. You stay independent; the studio carries the application.
  • You get credit and pay. Real credits on published work, fair rates on funded projects, ongoing collaboration where it fits.
