A hosted experience that makes taking part feel easy.

Every format opens the same way: the room moves together first. The idea comes after.

  1. Arrive

    WELI sets an inviting tone and gives people a way to join without needing to be good at anything.

  2. Lift

    Music, rhythm and simple movement create a shared shift without asking anyone to perform alone.

  3. Return

    The session closes on your theme, your next session or your final call to action.

Everyone joins from where they sit. Standing is optional, and every move is simple.

What organisers ask before booking.

Anything not covered here, ask in your enquiry. You will get a straight answer.

Do people have to dance?

No. Everyone can take part from their seat, and every move is optional. It feels like a warm-up the whole room shares.

Does anyone get brought on stage?

Nobody is brought on stage or singled out. WELI leads from the front and the room follows together.

Can it carry our event theme?

Yes. The Interlude and Keynote land your theme through a short story and song, and the Workshop builds on it. Name the theme when you enquire, and send any approved source material with the booking.

What do you need from the venue?

A PA that suits the room, one wireless headset or handheld microphone, a playback connection for backing tracks and a short soundcheck. A screen only if the concept uses visuals. You receive a one-page technical brief with the quote.

Is it accessible?

The session works seated, standing or with very little movement. Tell us about mobility, hearing, vision or sensory needs when you enquire, and the session is planned around them.

Where do you work, and can it run online?

WELI is based in Sydney and travels across Australia. Travel outside Sydney metro is quoted separately. In person is the default; online or hybrid sessions can be agreed in writing.

How does booking work?

Share the event, audience and moment. WELI recommends a format, usually within two business days. You receive a fixed quote and booking terms. The date is confirmed after written acceptance and the deposit or invoice payment.
