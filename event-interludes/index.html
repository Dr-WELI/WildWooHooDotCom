What organisers ask before booking.

Anything not covered here, ask in your enquiry. You will get a straight answer.

Do people have to dance?

No. Everyone can take part from their seat, and every move is optional. It feels like a warm-up the whole room shares.

Does anyone get brought on stage?

Nobody is brought on stage or singled out. WELI leads from the front and the room follows together.

Can it connect to our event theme?

Yes. Name the theme when you enquire, and WELI opens with a short line that links it to the song and the room.

What do you need from the venue?

A PA that suits the room, one wireless headset or handheld microphone, a playback connection for the backing track and a short soundcheck. You receive a one-page technical brief with the quote.

Is it accessible?

The session works seated, standing or with very little movement. Tell us about mobility, hearing, vision or sensory needs when you enquire, and the session is planned around them.

Where do you work, and what does travel cost?

WELI is based in Sydney and works across Australia, often in Canberra. Travel outside Sydney metro is quoted separately and shown in your written quote.

How does booking work?

Send the event, the audience and the moment. You get a reply within two business days with a fixed written quote and booking terms. The date is confirmed after written acceptance and the deposit or invoice payment.
