Engagement

Engagement and events

Live engagement that carries research to real audiences through music, story and performance. WELI delivers the work, WildWooHoo produces it, and every booking runs through the Dr WELI programme pages.

What you can book

WELI delivers the live work in person, so talks, workshops and school visits are booked on his site, drweli.com. WildWooHoo produces the work, and handles partnerships and commissions here.

School science incursion

An interactive science, music and movement session for a class, year level or whole school. Quoted per visit.

Conference keynote and energiser

A talk, or an energiser (a short session that gets a tired audience up and moving, a mid-afternoon reset) that opens, resets or closes your event. Quoted per event.

University and researcher workshop

Helps researchers turn their work into plain language, story and performance. Quoted per session or cohort.

Consultancy project

We shape and produce a piece of creative engagement to your brief. Quoted per project.

Creative Research Engagement Partner

A 6 to 12 month retainer; WELI works across your conferences, public engagement, research translation and staff development through the year. Quoted per month.

Original song or film commission

We turn your research or message into a finished song or short film for release. Quoted per project.

Sync licence

License an existing WELI track for your film, ad or campaign. Quoted per placement.

Where this has run

The work has been delivered and recognised across festivals, conferences and cultural institutions in Australia and internationally.

  • Floriade 2025, Canberra.
  • ACT National Science Week, 2024 and 2025, with Inspiring Australia ACT.
  • Encontro Anual de Etologia 2024, opening plenary, Brazil, with the song Cabra da Peste.
  • ANU HDR Research Festival.
  • Athens Sci-Co, the Athens Science Festival.
  • Guest lecture, ANU Centre for the Public Awareness of Science.
  • Falling Walls Engage 2025, Top 30, with the Science and the People programme.
  • Kangaroo Time, the AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024.

Looking for a school incursion instead? The Kangaroo Time Kids classroom programme books through the schools programme.

Questions

What can I book?

Four live science-engagement offerings: keynotes and conference energisers, science-art residencies, commissions and campaigns, festival and community engagement, and university research-communication and Dance Your PhD workshops. Each one books through the matching Dr WELI programme page at drweli.com/program.

Who is it for?

Conference and event organisers, science festivals and public science events, cultural institutions, councils, community programmes, and universities. Schools can book the Kangaroo Time Kids incursion through the schools programme.

Where do you deliver?

Across Greater Sydney and Canberra as a home base, and Australia-wide by arrangement. The work has also travelled internationally, including to Brazil and Greece.

Is it WELI or the studio?

WELI, Dr Weliton Menário Costa, is the artist, speaker and creative scientist who delivers the talks, performances and workshops. WildWooHoo is the studio that produces the work and partners on it. Speaking and performances are booked through Dr WELI at drweli.com.

How do I book?

Follow the link on each offering to its Dr WELI programme page and enquire there, or email hello@wildwoohoo.com and we will route you to the right programme.
