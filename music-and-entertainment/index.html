PhD-research-derived flagship. Song, music video, embodied choreography, and a diverse Canberra dance cast. Translating behavioural-ecology research on kangaroos into a high-energy multicultural pop object.

What it is

An interdisciplinary undertaking. Original song, full music video, embodied choreography, and a diverse Canberra dance cast translating PhD research on individual personality in 300+ eastern grey kangaroos into a multicultural pop-cultural object. Music, film, dance, science, in one piece.

The work

The song parallels kangaroo behavioural differences with the varying dance styles and aesthetics of the micro-communities living in Canberra. Differences lead to diversity became the project's core message and the lens for everything WildWooHoo has built since.

Why this work

“As a queer immigrant from a linguistically diverse developing country, I understand the challenges of feeling disconnected in certain environments.”

WELI, Australian National University, 29 February 2024

Source research

Dr WELI's PhD work on kangaroo personality, social environment, and maternal effects at the Australian National University. Peer-reviewed paper in Behavioral Ecology (2023): academic.oup.com/beheco/article/34/6/1002/7274023 ↗

[ + format + influences ]

An ever-growing album. The format borrows the indeterminate-growth pattern of kangaroos – new songs every year, for as long as the album keeps living.

What it is

WildWooHoo Studio's flagship music project. An ever-growing album, inspired by the indeterminate growth of kangaroos. The concept reaches across global ideas, but at its heart it is a celebration of us and nature as a unity. A living album that keeps growing, the way kangaroos do.

Core message and lens

A spark of hope. A joyful madness. A celebration of us and nature as one. The album carries the energy of big championships – the moments when human skill is put to the test, the world watches, and there is a winner. Music as the room where the question gets sung and felt, not argued.

The format – indeterminate growth, inspired by kangaroos

Kangaroos have indeterminate growth: their skeletons keep growing throughout their lives. EVER+GROW borrows that pattern. Three songs in year one. Three in year two. Three in year three. Then at least one new song every year after that, for as long as the album keeps living.

How it starts – three songs in 2026 / 2027

The arc follows change and choices – inspired by the changing climate and the natural world we belong to – moving through disconnection, reconnection, and our power to choose differently. Each song uses an animal narrator to bring the question close.

Research influence

The indeterminate-growth framing draws on macropod life-history research, including the published work of Marco Festa-Bianchet (Universite de Sherbrooke) on kangaroo growth and life-history. Research influence on the album's format only – not concept partner.

Credits

  • Writer and performer. WELI.
  • Producer and co-publisher. WildWooHoo Studio.
  • Producers, artists, vocalists. Drawn from the studio's creative register; named per track on release.

Upcoming 02 · In development · Services + products from KT

Kangaroo Time Legacy

Services and products that carry the KT precedent forward · anchored in diversity and inclusion

STATUS:in development SCOPE:music · show · books · school · merch · apps

More music. An animal show. Children's books. Classroom STEAM formats. Apparel and prints. A small app ecosystem. Live tours. All anchored in diversity and inclusion as method.

[ + legacy scope + roadmap ]

Services and products that carry the Kangaroo Time precedent forward. Anchored in diversity and inclusion as method, not just message.

What it is

A line of work extending Kangaroo Time across formats. Music and music videos, an animal-led narrative show, children's books, classroom materials, merchandise, a small ecosystem of apps, and live touring. Each carries the original work's anchor: diversity and inclusion through scientific storytelling, with non-human narrators carrying the question close.

In development

  • More music and video. Original songs and music videos in the Kangaroo Time lineage. Continues through EVER+GROW.
  • Animal show. An animal-led narrative format in design. The camp-meets-science energy of the flagship extended into a recurring show.
  • Children's books. Kangaroo Time Kids book series in development. Behavioural-ecology stories told through animal narrators for primary-school audiences.
  • School materials. Classroom STEAM formats and curriculum-aligned lesson plans, already delivered through Kangaroo Time Kids school visits.
  • Merchandise. Apparel, prints, and illustrations carrying the Kangaroo Time visual world. Launch planned through shop.wildwoohoo.com.
  • Apps and technology. A small in-house app ecosystem under the Dr-WELI org. Educator tools, interactive companions to the children's books, AR companions to the animal show, a public-engagement stack, and the shop infrastructure that ships the merch.
  • Live tours. Touring performances and public-engagement formats with multicultural ensembles. Built from the cast model that powered the original flagship.
  • Documentary and podcasts. Long-form audio and screen formats around the making of Kangaroo Time, the research it draws from, and the cast and collaborators across continents.

Why diversity and inclusion as anchor

The original Kangaroo Time cast brought together twelve dance styles drawn from multicultural Canberra. That casting was not decoration; it was the work. The legacy line carries that anchor forward into every product: diversity and inclusion in how we commission, cast, write, build, and ship.

Status

In design and development across all threads. Seeking partners across publishing, broadcast, retail, education, music, and live performance.
