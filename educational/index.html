Educational

Most children's media talks about animals. We make kids' formats that feel like them.

WildWooHoo's educational practice builds children's animated series, illustrated books, and play-based learning formats from the same source as the studio's music: behavioural research, animal lives, and the wild we all belong to.

Story first. Science underneath. Kids feel close to nature first, and notice they've learned something second.

Already proven: the kids-facing version of our flagship project, Kangaroo Time Kids, is used in school assemblies and STEAM classrooms.

Founded and led by Dr WELI, PhD ecologist, recording artist, and Falling Walls Engage 2025 Top 30 worldwide for transforming science into public culture.
