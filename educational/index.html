What kids
take away

Story · Play · Nature

Nature, imagination, and storytelling as entry points. Kids feel first — then notice they've learned something.

Outcome / 01

Curiosity about the natural world

Animals, ecosystems, behaviour, environmental change — kids start asking "how does that work?"

Outcome / 02

A sense of belonging

Recognising themselves as part of the animal kingdom — coexistence, individuality, diversity.

Outcome / 03

Emotional fluency

Around difference, identity, and what it feels like to live in changing environments.

Outcome / 04

Confidence to share

Joy in observing, asking, connecting — and telling the story back in their own voice.
