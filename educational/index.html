In development: Kanga-Kangaroo

Children's animated series · 26 × 11 min · concept locked, entering narrative development. Open to studio partners, broadcasters, streamers and children's media co-producers.

What if being different is exactly how you fit in?

Led by an expert in kangaroo behaviour.

Format: Children's animated series — 26 episodes, 11 minutes each. Playful, witty, humorous — a show for the whole family.

Logline: Follow Kanga, a fresh-out-of-the-pouch young, in her adventures exploring life on her own.

Creative team:

  • Weliton Menário Costa (Dr WELI) — Creator · Creative Director · Co-Showrunner (with the producer). PhD in Ecology and Evolution, transdisciplinary practitioner across science, arts, and culture.
  • Daniel Bury — Supervising Director (technical lead). Award-winning writer/director with experience across narrative, animation, digital arts, and emerging mediums.

Central theme: Kangaroo decisions emerge from an interplay between personality and social influence. Individuals differ in behaviour, but in groups they adjust to one another through a democratic process — all personalities contribute to a group response, with no bias toward age, gender, or extreme personalities. The series explores shared decisions and positive group outcomes, alongside chaos, challenge, and individual adventure.

“Let's have a nah-yeah of a kangaroo time, Bro!” says Kanga to her older brother, Bro. But she doesn't know Bro is her brother.

Approach: Grounded in real behavioural ecology, the series translates complex biological processes into engaging, culturally resonant, and fun storytelling.

Status: Concept and artistic direction developed. Next phase: narrative development. Open to animation studios, broadcast partners, streaming commissioners, and children's media co-producers.
