What we evolved for

WildWooHoo Studio is a production company for the animal kingdom, including us. The studio's intellectual core asks one question: what we evolved for, versus what we are putting ourselves into. We work from behavioural ecology and animal sociality, and we run two wings: Music and Entertainment for popular-culture reach, and Research and Educational for the science and learning underneath. Founded and led by Dr WELI.

ABN 61 239 662 726 Sydney | Canberra · Australia · Worldwide Founder Dr WELI (Weliton Menário Costa)

Meet the founder

Dr WELI - creative director, behavioural ecologist, recording artist

Brazilian born, Sydney based. Weliton Menário Costa designs cultural experiences, workshops, and programmes through music, movement, and storytelling, connecting science, performance, and public culture.

WildWooHoo is his creative studio: where a PhD on kangaroo personality (ANU, where he is now a CPAS Visiting Fellow) becomes songs, videos, animation, books, and public moments that reach far beyond the lab. The flagship work, Kangaroo Time, won Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024 and travelled through 238+ outlets worldwide. Falling Walls Engage 2025 Top 30, for transforming science into compelling public communication.

The lens stays the same across every form: animal behaviour, sociality, and evolution, refracted through witty songwriting, popular culture, traditions, and the ecosystems we share. The artist surface is named simply WELI. The founder, academic, and science-communicator surface is Dr WELI. Same person, two professional names for two industries.

How we
create

A four-step process underneath every WildWooHoo project. Animal behaviour and ecology first. Emotion second. Story third. Output last.

  1. Step 01

    Animal behaviour as muse

    We begin with natural behaviour, an animal society, an ecosystem, or an environmental feeling.

  2. Step 02

    Emotion as entry point

    We shape music, imagery, rhythm, and language so audiences feel first and reflect second.

  3. Step 03

    Interconnected storytelling

    Human and non-human worlds are placed in relation, so the work creates recognition and social meaning at the same time.

  4. Step 04

    Creative work with message

    The output becomes a song, music video, children's series, book, classroom format, or public moment, designed to travel and carry the impact.

Method: Living Wild

The studio's field-immersion artistic-residency framework. Go where wild animals are, embed with field scientists on their research, live the moment with the animals, and bring it home as music, movement, image, and sound. The groundwork every song, film, and learning programme grows from.

The work starts in the field.

What it is. Kangaroo Time came from years living alongside real kangaroos. Living Wild makes that the method: go where wild animals are, live the moment with them, and bring it home as creative work. Dr WELI embeds with field scientists on their research, then turns the lived experience of the animal into songs, films, photography, and field recordings that feed the studio's projects, and stand alone as wildlife storytelling.

Built with field scientists, not just about them. Their fieldwork gets cultural reach and documentation; the studio gets the lived truth of the animal. Two-way, every time. Australia first, then the wider world the science reaches.
