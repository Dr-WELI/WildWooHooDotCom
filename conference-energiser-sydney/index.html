Questions before you book in Sydney.

Do you add travel for a Sydney venue?

Not inside the Sydney metro area. WELI is based in Sydney, so the fee is A$1,200. For a venue further out, travel is its own line in the written quote.

Can it open the day as an ice breaker?

Yes. Placed first, it is an ice breaker the whole room does at once. It runs about ten minutes, so it fits before your opening keynote.

Does it suit a corporate audience?

Yes. People join from their seat or beside it, and every move is optional. Nobody is called up on stage.

What will our venue's AV team need?

A PA for the room, one wireless microphone, a line to play the backing track and a short soundcheck. The one-page brief comes with your quote, ready to forward.

Can people with limited mobility join in?

Yes. Every move works seated. Tell us about any access needs when you enquire, and the session is planned around them.

Do you also work in Canberra?

Yes, often. The Canberra page covers Canberra events, with travel from Sydney shown in the quote.
