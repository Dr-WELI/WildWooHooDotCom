The Creative Register

WildWooHoo works with a curated register of music, film, animation, photography, design and research collaborators, drawn on project by project as funded work comes in. Many of the people we have made work with are credited across our projects. If you would like to be considered for future collaborations, you are welcome to register your interest.

By expression of interest · reviewed on a rolling basis Engaged project by project · under contract Based in Sydney | Canberra · Australia · worldwide

How the
register
works

  • You register your interest. Share links to your work, your discipline, and where you are based.
  • We keep you on the register. The studio reviews the register as projects take shape. We look for craft and fit, not credentials.
  • Projects come to you. When a funded brief fits your discipline and location, we reach out. You opt in or pass.
  • We deliver together. Engagements are agreed project by project and contracted, with proper credit and fair rates on funded work.
  • The register is our capacity. Grant applications cite the register as part of WildWooHoo's delivery capacity. You stay independent; the studio carries the application.

Disciplines we
collaborate across

  • Music - producers, songwriters, vocalists and topliners. Alt-pop, Latin, electronic and genre-fluid especially.
  • Film & video - directors, cinematographers, editors for music video, documentary and short-form.
  • Animation & illustration - animators, illustrators and book designers across our screen and publishing work.
  • Movement - choreographers and dancers who translate animal behaviour into embodied performance.
  • Photography & styling - photographers, stylists, art directors, hair-and-make-up artists and models.
  • Research - behavioural ecologists and field scientists who consult on the science behind the work.
  • Writing & story - children's and editorial writers, plus short-form and social producers.

Members of the register see live project briefs - with scope, location and rates - inside the members' area. Geographies prioritised right now: Sydney | Canberra (priority), Brisbane, Melbourne (AU) · Berlin, London, Lisbon (EU) · São Paulo, Rio, Recife (BR). Other locations are welcome.

Register your
interest
