How to export PNG for TM submission:
  1. Pick the variant and background you want (controls above).
  2. Mac: press Cmd+Shift+4, then press Space, then click on the lockup frame. Or zoom your browser to 200-300% first for higher-resolution export.
  3. Cross-platform: Right-click the lockup area → "Inspect", select the .lockup-frame node, right-click → "Capture node screenshot" (Chrome DevTools).
  4. Or open wildwoohoo-lockup-color.svg directly in your browser, then File → Print → Save as PDF for vector output.
