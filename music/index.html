Music & Video

WildWooHoo's music and video practice sits inside the studio's wider work at the intersection of nature, music, and popular culture, with social impact at the centre.

We make original songs first. Pop, Latin, electronic, genre-fluid. Built around emotion, identity, and the feeling of a place.

Then we build the visual world around the song: music videos, casting, concept direction, identity. Every frame anchored to the song's meaning.

The flagship project, Kangaroo Time, won Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD in 2024 and travelled to 238+ outlets on six continents. Proof that culture and ecology belong together.

Founded and directed by Dr WELI, Sydney-based Brazilian-born music artist and PhD ecologist.
