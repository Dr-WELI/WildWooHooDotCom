WildWooHoo x WELI

The programme

WildWooHoo x WELI is an artist-led programme. WELI fronts it as its artist, voice and face, and he is also a behavioural ecologist and the studio's founder. It is the studio's living-world line, where his research on animal behaviour, sociality and evolution becomes music, books and school programmes. WildWooHoo produces and partners the work, and schools, festivals, funders and institutions can take it up directly.

The science

The work starts in the field. Dr WELI is a behavioural ecologist. His peer-reviewed PhD research studied the personality and social behaviour of wild eastern grey kangaroos at ANU, covering individual personality, social environment and maternal effects. His earlier research looked at goat behaviour. This is the science the rest of the programme grows from.

The full academic profile sits at drweli.com/academic.

The music

Two songs grew from that research. Kangaroo Time is the kangaroo study turned into music and film. It won the AAAS Dance Your PhD Global Winner 2024 award. Cabra da Peste was commissioned for and performed at the Etologia 2024 plenary in Brazil, on the theme of creativity and science unifying communities. Both play on a double meaning: the kangaroos have their mates, and the song keeps its own kangaroo time.

These two songs are the seeds of sci pop, the studio's coined genre. The genre, its peers and how it works are set out on the Sci pop page. You can hear both songs on the Music and Entertainment page.

The books and the classroom

The same research feeds children's media and school programmes. Kangaroo Time Kids puts kangaroo behaviour into music, dance and embodied learning, so children take part with their bodies and meet the science through play. It is the kids version of Kangaroo Time, and it is in delivery now through Dr WELI's schools engagement programme.

Children's books grounded in the behavioural-ecology research are in development. Where these works live, and the wider research and educational scope around them, is set out on the Research and Educational page. Merch and character goods sit in the shop.

Home ground

This programme is where the studio keeps its living-world, animal-behaviour home ground. WildWooHoo produces and WELI leads. The work holds a quiet idea throughout: we are still part of the animal kingdom. Studying kangaroos and goats is also a way of studying ourselves.

This wing is led by Dr WELI, a Sydney-based behavioural ecologist and recording artist. His academic and engagement work is documented at drweli.com. To work with the programme, start a conversation.
