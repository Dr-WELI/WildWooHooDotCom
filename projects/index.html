Project 01 · Flagship · Released 2024

Kangaroo Time

Song + music video + dance · Award-winning

wildwoohoo.com/projects/#kangaroo-time

Concept developed: 2022–2023 · Released: February 2024 · © Weliton Menário Costa / WildWooHoo. All rights reserved.

Concept, credits & press

Source: Dr WELI's PhD research on kangaroo personality, social environment, and maternal effects (ANU).

Form: Original song, full music video, embodied dance, a single integrated cultural object.

Concept: The work parallels kangaroo behavioural differences with the varying dance styles and aesthetics of the micro-communities living in Canberra, Australia's capital, touching on diversity, individuality, and how groups make space for difference.

Thesis: Diversity, individuality, and coexistence, what animal societies teach us about our own.

Impact: Winner, Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024. 145K+ video views. Featured on Science, NPR, Smithsonian, The Guardian, ABC, BBC, O Globo. Used by schools and researchers internationally. See the full media coverage ↗

Photography & production stills of Kangaroo Time used throughout this site by Nic Vevers.

Project 02 · In development · Songwriting phase

Benevolence

3-track EP + trilogy music video · Working title: CHOICES / CHANGE · Seeking grant partners

Photo by Weliton Menário Costa.

wildwoohoo.com/projects/#benevolence → Open calls for this team

Concept developed: since 2025 · First published: 2026-05-21 (WildWooHoo) · © Weliton Menário Costa / WildWooHoo. All rights reserved.

Concept & tracks

Working title: CHOICES / CHANGE — three interconnected themes: disconnection, memory, and benevolence.

Core message: If everything changes, choices can change too. The choices humans have already made have shaped the planet's future — and we can choose again. Reconnection is the throughline, and the heart of what this studio is for.

Approach: Rather than reaching for fear-based narratives or pure scientific communication, this project focuses on emotional reconnection. It bridges human inner experience and environmental awareness through metaphor, storytelling, and sensory songwriting.

Symbolic mirrors: Each track uses elements of nature as mirrors of human relationships. Water represents impermanence and emotional flow. Butterflies represent transformation and becoming. Birds (parrots) represent memory, echo, and forgotten connection.

Track 1 — Disconnection: Sits with disconnection as an emotion. The feeling of being unmoored from your own sensations, your community, and the living world — named, not avoided. The track sets up the human–nature disconnect that the rest of the EP works to close.

Track 2 — Memory: Focuses on memory and recognition. Draws parallels between environmental degradation and personal experiences of emotional neglect, using memory as a bridge to empathy.

Track 3 — Benevolence: The closure. Reconnection grounded in care, softness, and conscious choice — no guilt, no shaming. An invitation to choose again, gently, in the direction of kindness and awareness.

Artistic approach: Contemporary pop songwriting · nature-based metaphorical storytelling · cross-cultural influences (Latin, global pop, electronic textures) · science-informed concepts translated into emotional narratives.

Outcome: Music that resonates with audiences who may not initially engage with climate discourse, by connecting environmental awareness to familiar human emotions — love, loss, memory, and care.

Status: Songwriting in progress. Production has not yet begun — this is the phase Dr WELI is currently writing grant applications for. Open to label partners, grant co-applicants, video production co-financing, and screening commissioners.

Project 03 · Released

Cabra da Peste (G.O.A.T.)

Original song · Latin/Brazilian musical identity · Creativity and science unifying communities

wildwoohoo.com/projects/#cabra-da-peste → Open calls for this team

Concept developed: 2023–2024 · Released: 2024 · © Weliton Menário Costa / WildWooHoo. All rights reserved.

Concept, credits & funding

Source: Dr WELI's Honours thesis on goat social hierarchy, with the special participation of his Honours supervisor in Brazil. Science of competition, dominance, and individuality — refracted through Brazilian rural culture.

Form: Original song with Brazilian/Latin musical identity. An interconnected narrative mixing female goat dominance, female empowerment, and the storytelling of Brazil's sertão.

Concept: Creativity and science unifying communities. Under the theme “Path Forward in Animal Behaviour Communication,” Dr WELI was invited to open the Encontro Anual de Etologia 2024 conference with a keynote on the role of creativity in Ethology.

Thesis: Hierarchy, conflict, and what individuality looks like when survival is the prize — told as a song the community can sing.

Status: Released worldwide on Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, YouTube Music.

Project 04 · In development · Children's animation

Kanga-Kangaroo

Children's animated series · 26 × 11 min · Seeking production partners

wildwoohoo.com/projects/#kanga-kangaroo → Open calls for this team

Concept developed: since 2025 · First published: 2026-05-21 (WildWooHoo) · © Weliton Menário Costa & Daniel Bury / WildWooHoo. All rights reserved.

Concept, creative team & format

What if being different is exactly how you fit in?

Led by an expert in kangaroo behaviour.

Format: Children's animated series — 26 episodes, 11 minutes each. Playful, witty, humorous — a show for the whole family.

Logline: Follow Kanga, a fresh-out-of-the-pouch young, in her adventures exploring life on her own.

Creative team:

  • Weliton Menário Costa (Dr WELI) — Creator · Creative Director · Co-Showrunner (with the producer). PhD in Ecology and Evolution, transdisciplinary practitioner across science, arts, and culture.
  • Daniel Bury — Supervising Director (technical lead). Award-winning writer/director with experience across narrative, animation, digital arts, and emerging mediums.

Central theme: Kangaroo decisions emerge from an interplay between personality and social influence. Individuals differ in behaviour, but in groups they adjust to one another through a democratic process — all personalities contribute to a group response, with no bias toward age, gender, or extreme personalities. The series explores shared decisions and positive group outcomes, alongside chaos, challenge, and individual adventure.

“Let's have a nah-yeah of a kangaroo time, Bro!” says Kanga to her older brother, Bro. But she doesn't know Bro is her brother.

Approach: Grounded in real behavioural ecology, the series translates complex biological processes into engaging, culturally resonant, and fun storytelling.

Status: Concept and artistic direction developed. Next phase: narrative development. Open to animation studios, broadcast partners, streaming commissioners, and children's media co-producers.

Project 05 · Released · Visual translation of PhD research

The Photography Project

Photography + models · A visual companion to the kangaroo behaviour research

wildwoohoo.com/projects/#photography-models → Open calls for this team

Shot: October 2023 · Released: 2024 · © Weliton Menário Costa / WildWooHoo. All rights reserved.

Concept & credits

Source: Dr WELI's PhD findings on kangaroo personality, social environment, and maternal effects, the same body of behavioural ecology research that drives Kangaroo Time.

Form: A staged photography series. Each model stands in for a finding, an axis of personality, a social role, a behavioural pattern. The photographs sit on the WildWooHoo site as the brand's photographic memory.

Thesis: Behavioural ecology becomes a portrait series. You see the science before you read it.

Where you've seen it: The images rolling through the main showreel on the WildWooHoo home page, and across the music, educational, and impact pages, are from The Photography Project. Photography by Tony Oliver and Reid Workman.

Status: Released. Photos used across WildWooHoo's home, music, and educational pages.

Project 06 · Active · Educational extension

Kangaroo Time Kids

School format · Primary & lower-secondary STEAM

wildwoohoo.com/projects/#kangaroo-time-kids → Open calls for this team

Concept developed: since 2024 · Active & bookable: 2025– · © Weliton Menário Costa / WildWooHoo. All rights reserved.

Concept & research base

Source: The flagship Kangaroo Time work, adapted for school assemblies and STEAM classrooms.

Form: Live presentation, embodied workshop, and screening-friendly video, children meet kangaroo behaviour through music, movement, and dance.

Thesis: Embodied learning of scientific method and animal behaviour, kids feel the work before they're asked to think it.

Status: Active. Bookable for schools, festivals, and STEAM programs. Open to education-sector funders and curriculum partners.

Built from the same research base as Kangaroo Time: fieldwork from the Holsworth Wildlife Research Endowment & Ecological Society of Australia, student scholarship from The Australian National University, research program from NSERC CRSNG (Canada).
