WildWooHoo originals

Originals, not commissions

The studio owns the question and builds the world around it.

A commissioned project begins with a partner's brief. An original begins here—with a scientific idea, a relationship with nature, or a human question we want to carry into popular culture. Partners can fund, host, license, publish or help produce it without turning it into client work.

The work so far

Song · music film · dance · 2024

Kangaroo Time

Dr WELI's research on personality and social behaviour in wild eastern grey kangaroos became a pop song, film and multicultural Canberra dance cast. The work connects behavioural difference with the idea that difference makes diversity possible.

AAAS Dance Your PhD Overall Winner 2024 · 238+ media features across five continents.

Song · live performance · 2024

Cabra da Peste (G.O.A.T.)

A Portuguese-language sci pop song built from WELI's undergraduate research on goats. It premiered at the opening plenary of the Encontro Anual de Etologia 2024 in Brazil.
