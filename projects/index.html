The projects we build

WildWooHoo isn't a service studio. We make our own songs, music videos, animated series, books, and public moments, each built from behavioural and ecological research, designed to travel, and owned by the studio. This is the catalogue.

Project 01 · Flagship · Released 2024

Kangaroo Time

Song + music video + dance · Award-winning

Source: Dr WELI's PhD research on kangaroo personality, social environment, and maternal effects (ANU).

Form: Original song, full music video, embodied dance, a single integrated cultural object.

Thesis: Diversity, individuality, and coexistence, what animal societies teach us about our own.

Impact: Winner, Science Magazine's Dance Your PhD 2024. 145K+ video views. Featured on Science, NPR, Smithsonian, The Guardian, ABC, BBC, O Globo. Used by schools and researchers internationally.

Project 02 · In development · 2026

Benevolence

3-song EP + trilogy music video · Seeking partners

Source: Environmental change, memory, and choice. The science of habitat shift, range loss, and what it means to live in a changing place.

Thesis: Belonging in changing environments, for animals, for migrants, for all of us.

Cabra da Peste (G.O.A.T.)
